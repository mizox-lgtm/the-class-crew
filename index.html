Study Smarter

Small habits make a real difference.

🗓️ Plan your week

Choose one clear goal for each study session and leave time to review.

✍️ Practise actively

Close your notes and try exercises before checking the answers.

🔁 Review regularly

Revisit difficult ideas after a day, then again later in the week.

“Small steps every day lead to big results.”
